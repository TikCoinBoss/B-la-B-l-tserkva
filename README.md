# Біла Церква / Bílá Cerkev

Minecraft-style 3D browser game: a block copy of the village Bila Tserkva (Zakarpattia, Ukraine) with its real streets, the Tisa river and the border.
Works on a phone and on a computer, the interface is in Ukrainian, Czech and Romanian.

A second map is included: the right bank of the Apshytsia river in Serednie Vodiane (Střední Vodiané / Apșa de Mijloc). Switch villages on the title screen or in the pause menu; direct link: add `#vodiane` to the address.

**Play:** open the GitHub Pages address of this repository (Settings → Pages) in a browser.

## Controls
- Computer: WASD to walk, mouse to look, Space to jump (twice to fly), left/right click to break/place, **P** for places and street teleport.
- Phone: left thumb to walk, right thumb to look, on-screen buttons for jump, break and place.

## Data and credits
- Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL): https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
- 3D engine: three.js (MIT licence), embedded in `index.html`.
- Heights, house colours and roofs are simplified; only a few key buildings follow real photos. No Google imagery is included.
- Serednie Vodiane: roads, river and forests were redrawn by hand from a satellite view, the houses are placed approximately (not surveyed), terrain heights come from the Copernicus DEM (modified, served by Open-Meteo) and are compressed vertically.
